01 / THE BEGINNING

Our story started in 2021.

Royal Treats began with a simple idea: make the ordinary feel special through sweet little moments. From a quick after-school treat to a box of mini donuts shared with family, we believe dessert is about more than flavour — it's about the smiles that follow.

Our roots are in Thabazimbi, and our menu celebrates playful favourites that people love to come back for.
